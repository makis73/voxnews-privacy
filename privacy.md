VoxNews App Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 
This privacy policy ("Policy") describes how VoxNews ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").
1. Information We Collect
1.1. Personal Information
We may collect the following types of personal information from you:
Account Information: Your name, email address, username, password, and profile picture when you create an account.
Contact Information: Your phone number, postal address, or other contact details you provide to us directly.
Communications: The content of any messages, reviews, or other communications you send to us or post within the App.
1.2. Usage Information
We may collect information about how you use the App, including:
Content Interactions: The news articles, audio content, and other features you access, view, listen to, or interact with.
Engagement Metrics: How you engage with the content and features, such as the time spent, shares, comments, and likes.
Device Interactions: Your device's hardware model, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers.
Location Data: Your approximate location, if you enable location services on your device.
1.3. Automatically Collected Information
We may automatically collect certain information when you use the App, such as:
Log Data: Information about your device, browser, and usage of the App, including IP address, device identifiers, and browser type.
Analytics Data: Information about how you use and navigate the App, which pages you visit, and how long you spend on each page.
Crash Reports: Information about any errors or crashes that occur while using the App.
2. How We Use Your Information
We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:
Provide and Improve the App: To deliver the App's features and functionality, personalize your experience, and enhance the overall quality of the App.
Personalize Content: To understand your interests and preferences and provide you with more relevant news, audio content, and recommendations.
Enable User Interactions: To allow you to engage with other users, such as by commenting on articles or sharing content.
Communicate with You: To respond to your inquiries, send you updates about the App, and provide customer support.
Analyze Usage: To monitor and analyze how the App is used, identify trends, and evaluate the effectiveness of our features and content.
Protect the App: To investigate and prevent any fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity related to the App.
Comply with Legal Obligations: To meet any legal, regulatory, or governmental requirements.
3. How We Share Your Information
We may share your information in the following circumstances:
Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, analytics, customer support, and content moderation.
With Your Consent: We may share your information with third parties if you have given us your consent to do so.
Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information to law enforcement, government authorities, or third parties if we believe it is necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect our rights, the rights of others, or the security and integrity of the App.
Business Transfers: We may share your information in connection with a corporate transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers or marketing companies.
4. Your Rights and Choices
4.1. Accessing and Updating Your Information
You can access, review, and update your personal information by logging into your account and modifying the information in your profile settings.
4.2. Deleting Your Information
You can request the deletion of your personal information by contacting us at privacy@voxnews.com. We will make reasonable efforts to honor your request, subject to any legal or regulatory obligations.
4.3. Opting Out of Communications
You can opt-out of receiving promotional emails or push notifications from us by adjusting your notification preferences in your account settings or by following the unsubscribe instructions in the communications you receive.
4.4. Controlling Content Visibility
You can control the visibility of your profile and the content you post within the App by adjusting your privacy and sharing settings.
5. Data Retention and Security
5.1. Data Retention
We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by law. We may retain certain usage data and anonymized information for analytical and improvement purposes.
5.2. Data Security
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no security measures are perfect, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.
6. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the effective date at the top of this document and, in some cases, providing additional notice (such as by sending you an email or displaying a prominent notice within the App).
7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@voxnews.com.
